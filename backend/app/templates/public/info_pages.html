Privacy Policy & Patient Data Protection

Rumi Dental Care treats all patient demographic details, medical histories, FDI dental charts, digital X-rays, prescriptions, and billing statements as strictly confidential medical records.

1. Patient-Level Data Isolation

Every patient query is scoped on the server to the authenticated account. No patient can view or download another patient's appointments, X-rays, prescriptions, or invoices.

2. Private Clinical Document Storage

Uploaded dental radiographs and clinical documents are stored outside public static directories and streamed only after server-side authorization and audit logging.

3. Public Testimonials & Consent

Patient reviews are moderated prior to publication and never disclose clinical diagnoses, treatment histories, phone numbers, or private identifiers.
